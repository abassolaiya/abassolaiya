## 🧠 Engineering Philosophy

I believe great engineering goes beyond writing code.

My approach is built around:

- **Problem First:** Understand the business problem before choosing the technology.
- **Scalable Architecture:** Build systems that can evolve as business requirements grow.
- **Maintainable Code:** Prioritize readability, modularity, and long-term maintainability.
- **Security by Design:** Consider security, data integrity, and reliability from the beginning.
- **Continuous Learning:** Stay curious, experiment, and improve engineering practices.
- **Business Impact:** Measure engineering success by the value delivered to users.
